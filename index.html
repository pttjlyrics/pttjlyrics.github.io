PTTJ Studio

Lyric videos and an animated office series made by a small creator team.

PTTJ Studio is the private production tool behind the creator accounts @.pttj (lyric videos) and @pttjstudio (an animated comedy series about an AI-powered office).

The tool prepares videos on the creator's own computer. With the TikTok integration, finished videos are sent to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts. The creator reviews, edits and publishes every single video manually in the TikTok app. Nothing is posted automatically, and the tool is used only for the creator's own accounts.

Deutsch: PTTJ Studio ist das private Produktions-Werkzeug hinter den Kanälen @.pttj und @pttjstudio. Fertige Videos landen als Entwurf im eigenen TikTok-Konto; jedes Video wird von Hand geprüft und selbst gepostet.
