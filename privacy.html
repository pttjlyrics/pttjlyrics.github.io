Privacy Policy

PTTJ Studio · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Who we are

PTTJ Studio is a private content-preparation tool operated by the owner of the TikTok accounts @.pttj and @pttjstudio. It is used only for these own accounts and is not offered to the public.

What data we access

When the owner connects his TikTok account, TikTok provides an access token and basic profile information (display name and avatar, scope user.info.basic). With the scope video.upload the tool uploads the owner's own finished videos to his TikTok account as drafts.

We do not access followers, messages, comments, contacts, location or any data of other TikTok users.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only locally on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to any other service. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

Third parties, tracking, advertising

No data is shared with third parties. This website and the tool use no tracking, no cookies for analytics and no advertising.

Deletion

Revoking the app permission in TikTok ends access immediately. Locally stored tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected in the tool. On request, all stored data is deleted.
